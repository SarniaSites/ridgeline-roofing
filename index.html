Re-roofing

We strip the old covering, check what’s underneath and put on a new roof in slate or clay tile, finished with matching ridges and verges.

What’s included

  • Old slates or tiles stripped and cleared
  • Timbers checked before anything goes back on
  • Breathable membrane and new treated battens
  • Slate or clay tile, with ridges and verge closers to match
  • Roof windows fitted neatly into the new covering

Roof repairs

We put right slipped and broken slates, tired flashings and the details around chimneys, dormers and roof windows.

What’s included

  • Slipped, cracked or missing slates and tiles
  • Flashings around chimneys and dormers
  • Ridges and verges re-set
  • Making good around roof windows

Low-pitch roofs

A shallow roof needs materials made for it. Where a low-pitch roof has failed because the wrong covering was used, we recover it with low-pitch slate and felt for a durable, weatherproof finish.

What’s included

  • Failed covering taken off
  • Low-pitch roofing felt
  • Low-pitch slate, laid to suit the angle of the roof
  • Roof windows and edges detailed to keep the weather out
Ask about a low-pitch roof

Strip-outs & restoration

On older roofs we strip right back to the rafters, old internal insulation included, so the structure can be checked properly. Sound original timbers are kept and the roof is rebuilt from there.

What’s included

  • Full strip back to the rafters
  • Old insulation and debris removed
  • Original timbers inspected and kept where sound
  • Re-insulated, felted, battened and re-covered
Talk to us about a restoration

How a re-roof runs

The order of work on a full re-roof, once the scaffold is up.

  2. 1

    Strip back

    The old slates or tiles come off and the roof is cleared down to the rafters.

  3. 2

    Check the timbers

    With everything open, the structure is checked so the new roof goes onto sound timber.

  4. 3

    Insulate, felt & batten

    Insulation goes in between the rafters, followed by a breathable membrane and new battens.

  5. 4

    Slate or tile

    The new covering goes on and is finished with ridges, verges and any roof windows.
