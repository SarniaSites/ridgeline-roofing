How a roof goes back on

A full re-roof, from the scaffold up, in five stages.

Scaffold upStage 0 of 5

  1. 1

    Strip it back

    Slates, felt and old insulation come off, down to the rafters. Clearing old insulation is painstaking, but it's the only way to strip a roof back properly.

  2. 2

    Check the timbers

    With the roof open, every rafter gets checked. On our latest strip the original timbers were in good condition, so they stayed.

  3. 3

    Membrane and battens

    New roofing membrane goes over the rafters, then treated battens are set out across it at the right spacing for the slate or tile.

  4. 4

    Slates or tiles

    Courses go on from the eaves up: natural slate, low-pitch slate or terracotta clay tile, depending on the roof.

  5. 5

    Ridge and verges

    Matching ridges and verge closers go on last, and the roof is topped off. Done and dusted.
